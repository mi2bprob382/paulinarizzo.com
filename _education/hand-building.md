## 1️⃣ Individual Private Hand-Building (3 Hours)

> **One-on-one** — start with a studio tour & optional meditation, then learn pinch, coil, and slab methods to build your own project.

### Session Flow
1. **Studio Tour & Grounding** (10 min) — optional brief meditation to connect with the material
2. **Pinch Technique Mug** (30 min) — perfect beginner form to feel the clay
3. **Project Development** (1 hr 20 min) — combine coils, slabs, and pinch to create a personal piece
4. **Surface Decoration** (20 min) — texture tools, colored slips, carving
5. **Solo Practice** (1 hr) — independent work with tutor available for questions

### What You'll Learn
- Wedging & preparing clay
- Pinch pots → cups, bowls
- Coil building → vessels, sculptural forms
- Slab construction → boxes, plates, geometric forms
- Joining techniques & structural integrity
- Surface decoration: texture, slip trailing, carving, colored slips
