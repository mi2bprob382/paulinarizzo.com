## Hand building classes

### Cost

**Single class fee: MX$350**
{: .text-center}
Includes:
* 1.5 kg of natural clay
* 2 hour class
* Low-temperature firing for the pottery you create in the class (without glaze)

**Monthly fee: MX$1200**
{: .text-center}
Includes:
* 1.5 kg of natural clay each class
* Four 2-hour classes
* Low-temperature firing for the pottery you create in the class (without glaze)

**Private Classes**
{: .text-center}

If Tuesdays don't fit in your schedule or if you would like to use the pottery wheel, you can [book a private pottery class](/education/san-agustin-etla-private-ceramics-classes/).

### What to know

* Guidance and necessary tools are included
* Beginner-level bilingual classes
* Tuesdays 3:00-5:00 P.M.
* Class size is limited to 6 people
* Classes are held at my studio in San Agustín Etla, Oaxaca, Mexico
* Please bring comfortable clothes that you can get dirty

### About firing your pottery

I normally fire student pottery in a collective manner using a large kiln. Once a quantity of work large enough to fill the kiln is complete and dry, I then fire it all together. As a result, wait time to fill the kiln and fire your pieces can reach up to two months. If you need a faster turnaround for your class pottery, please [contact me](mailto:paulinarizzo.s@gmail.com) to assess feasibility and cost for your timeline.
